## Passport requirements

The passport must be valid for at least six months from the date of arrival in India and must have at least two blank pages. The applicant must travel on the same passport used in the e-Visa application. If the visa was granted on an old passport, carry that old passport too.

## When to apply

Apply at least four days before arrival. Mission pages disagree on the earliest date you can apply (30, 60, 120 or 180 days are all stated). Confirm the current window on indianvisaonline.gov.in before relying on it. Pay the fee at least four days before travel.

## Who cannot use the e-Visa

Holders of diplomatic or official passports, holders of travel documents, and people endorsed on someone else's passport cannot use the e-Visa. People of Pakistani origin must apply for a regular visa at an Indian mission. Several mission pages state a limit of two e-Visas per calendar year.

## On arrival

Biometrics are captured at immigration. Travellers arriving from a country affected by yellow fever must carry a Yellow Fever vaccination card.
